Connect to Freedeck on iOS

To connect to your Freedeck, all you have to do is scan the QR code. You'll instantly connect to your computer from there.

Or, connect using my.freedeck.app
  • Open Safari and go to
  • Type in code Please accept to use this service... and press Connect.
  • When you see your Freedeck, press Share at the bottom.
  • Press Add To Home Screen
Or, manually connect using your local IP
  • Follow every instruction, but instead of going to , go to http://:5754 on your device.
  • You won't need to press Connect, as this is directly taking you to your Freedeck.

This will automatically close when your device is connected.
