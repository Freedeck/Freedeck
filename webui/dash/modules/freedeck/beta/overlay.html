Freedeck Overlay is BETA software!

Press ALT+SHIFT+BACKSPACE to activate the layout editor.

The Layout Editor

From there, you'll see a grid appear on your screen.
It's used to help you differentiate desktop from Overlay.
Right click an empty spot to add a module there, drag the modules,
and right click to edit them.
Modules are loosely implemented, meaning that one's
capabilities and settings won't be the exact same for a different one.
For example, the Spotify plugin's overlay lets you change the
background transparency, while this one doesn't.

Overlay is based on Freedeck Dash.
